# Teams and attendance

## 3. Teams, captain, numbering and seating

- **3.1 Line-up.** 3 players per partido; a 4th can be registered as a substitute. The line-up only changes between partidos (clause XI of the Terms and Conditions). There are no tactical changes once the partido has started. If the venue does not allow more than 3 per team, the substitute does not play that phase.
- **3.2 Captain.** One per partido; only changed between partidos. Keeps the role even after tapar or irse al mazo (may file claims, but not make plays). If they leave due to force majeure, the referee may authorize a new captain.
- **3.3 Captain's duties.**
  - Confirm the line-up and the reserved numbering 1-2-3.
  - Be the only one who formally requests the referee's intervention.
  - Receive and communicate the referee's decisions.
  - Be the spokesperson for collective decisions (their decision binds the team; it does not affect individual plays or the decisions within duelos).
  - Decide whether to continue or repeat the deal when a card meant for their team is exposed (6.1).
  - Validate or dispute the result in the official system.
- **3.4 Reserved numbering.** Before the partido, each team secretly assigns the numbers 1, 2 and 3 and tells the referee. It stays fixed for the whole partido. Pica-pica always pits 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2 and 3 vs 3. A deliberate attempt to alter the pairings → verbal or disciplinary warning; fraud falls under ch. 19.
- **3.5 Seating.** Alternate, with players of the same number **facing each other**. Fixed for the whole partido. Deliberately altering it → disciplinary warning; doing it again after a referee's correction → possible disqualification.

## 4. Attendance, breaks and devices

- **4.1 Attendance.** All 3 eligible players must be ready at the official time. At **+5 min** → possible disciplinary warning for delay. At **+10 min** → loss by absence. The referee (or the Chief Referee) may waive this if the delay is the organization's, due to force majeure or an exceptional cause.
- **4.2 Devices.** No personal phones on the table and no headphones, except for the devices authorized to enter the score (only for that, with notifications silenced). A smartwatch may be used as a watch, but not to read messages; receiving a notification is not an offence, opening it is. Refusing to put the phone away or doing it again → disciplinary warning. Accessing betting sites is forbidden.
- **4.3 Leaving the table.** Requires the referee's authorization; except in an emergency, not during a mano. Without justification → disciplinary warning. During breaks no devices are used (unless an exception is authorized) and no game information is received.
- **4.4 Force majeure.** The referee may suspend play. With proven force majeure, they may authorize the registered substitute to come in at the **same number and position** (never to replace a disqualified player or as a tactical change). If it prevents settling the mano in progress, they may annul it without awarding points.
