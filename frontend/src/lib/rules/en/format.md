## 1. General provisions and competition format

- **1.1 Purpose.** Rules of play, conduct, refereeing and result recording for Se Pica TSA partidos. They form part of the contract together with the Terms and Conditions, the Particular Conditions (_Condiciones Particulares_) of each edition and the Privacy Policy (Organizer: Brand Flow S.A.S.). On matters unrelated to play, refereeing or discipline, the Terms and Conditions and the Particular Conditions prevail.
- **1.2 Format.** Two teams of three players; six players seated alternately.
- **1.3 Single partido.** To 30 points; the first to get there wins.
- **1.4 Malas and buenas.** Points 1–15 = malas; 16–30 = buenas.
- **1.5 Score cap.** The score stops at 30; if a play would give more, only the points needed to reach 30 are added.
- **1.6 No flor.** With three cards of the same suit, only two are used for the envido.
- **1.7 Same rules** for televised, recorded and non-televised partidos (cameras only change the evidence available).
- **1.8 Bracket and phases.** The Particular Conditions of each edition define the groups, bracket, phases, places per stage and invited teams. Classification and administrative results are governed by 1.13 and 1.14.
- **1.9 Nature.** The result depends only on sporting performance; the Organizer does not run any betting.
- **1.10 Registration.** It pays for the organization; it is not a bet and does not fund prizes.
- **1.11 Prizes.** Fixed, defined before registration opens and funded by the Organizer.
- **1.12 Betting ban** for players, captains, coaching staff and immediate family. It is an integrity offence (ch. 18) and may lead to disqualification (ch. 19).
- **1.13 Classification criteria.** See _Group tiebreakers_, below.
- **1.14 Administrative results.** See _Result types_, below. Earlier partidos of a disqualified team stand.

## Result types (1.14)

| Type                    | When                                                                          | Record                        |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Sporting                | A team reaches 30                                                             | Actual score (e.g. 30–22)     |
| Win by Absence          | The rival does not show up within 10 min (4.1)                                | Administrative, **not** 30–0  |
| Win by Disqualification | The rival is disqualified                                                     | Administrative                |
| Win by Abandonment      | The rival refuses or is unable to continue (without authorized force majeure) | Administrative                |
| Win by Sanction         | The rival loses by sanction without being disqualified (e.g. 2nd warning)     | Administrative                |
| Double Disqualification | Both disqualified in the same partido                                         | No winner; neither adds a win |

For the standings, an administrative win is worth **30 points for**, and its point difference is the **average difference** of the wins the beneficiary actually played in the same phase (or 0 if it has none) (1.13).

## Group tiebreakers (1.13)

In this order:

1. Most partidos won.
2. Best point difference (sum of points scored − conceded, partido by partido).
3. Most points scored.
4. Head-to-head (if the tied teams played each other and that settles the tie).
5. Draw by Se Pica TSA.

The same criteria rank group runners-up against each other, if the Particular Conditions qualify them.
