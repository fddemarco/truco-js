# Cantos: envido and truco

## 10. Verbal declarations and answers

- **10.1 First valid expression.** If teammates say different things, the **first** complete, clear and audible regulation expression counts. Compound expressions are taken as a whole ("no quiero" does not contain a "quiero").
- **10.2 Context.** In active play, every regulation expression takes effect even if said as a joke, in a question or by mistake (except by players of a future duelo de pica-pica or during a formal suspension by the referee).
- **10.3 Immediate pardon.** An accidental expression can be cancelled immediately and unambiguously if it is a minor irregularity: in a mano redonda, with both captains agreeing; in pica-pica, the two players of the duelo. If play continues knowingly, it is validated. Holding back a claim to use it depending on how the mano turns out is forbidden.
- **10.4 Valid answers in a mano redonda.**
  - Accept: `quiero`, `se quiere`, `queremos`.
  - Reject: `no quiero`, `no se quiere`, `no queremos`.
  - **Not** valid answers: "al mazo", "dale", "sí", "vamos", "jugamos".
- **10.5 Valid answers in pica-pica** (individual): `quiero`, `se quiere`, `no quiero`, `no se quiere`. "Queremos" and "no queremos" are **not** valid.

## 11. Envido

- **11.1 Calculation.**
  - Cards 1 to 7 are worth their number; figuras (10, 11, 12) are worth 0.
  - Two cards of the same suit: **20 + the sum** of their values.
  - Without two of the same suit: the highest numbered card counts.
  - With three of the same suit (there is no flor): the two that make the right tanto are used (in practice, the two highest).
  - Possible range: 0–7 without a pair, 20–33 with a pair.
- **11.2 Timing.** From the end of the deal until the **last player of the first ronda** plays their first card (with the special rules on turns and pending cantos).
- **11.3 Invitations.** Envido, real envido and falta envido, combined as in Anexo B.
- **11.4 Declaration.** Once querido, the **mano** declares first, then the others in table order. In pica-pica only the two players of the duelo declare (the one who is mano between them starts). The **first complete number** said counts ("treinta y uno" is a single number). It cannot be corrected.
- **11.5 "Son buenas".** Concedes that the player does not beat the rival tanto. It includes teammates who should have declared earlier and stayed silent. It does not cancel a number a teammate already said.
- **11.6 Ties.** Between rivals with the same tanto, the one who is **mano** relative to the other wins. If several teammates declared the same winning tanto, it is credited to whichever of them is mano.
- **11.7 Proof.** The winner shows the cards needed to prove **exactly** the number (they need not show more).
  - Mano redonda: the rival may claim until the **mazo is cut** for the next mano. If the claim is in time and the winner cannot prove it → **all the points actually at stake in that mano** go to the rival.
  - No claim before the cut → everything is validated.
- **11.8 False declaration by the envido loser.** They do not have to show their cards. If the cards are legitimately exposed in time and prove they did not have what they said → **all the points of the mano** go to the rival.
- **11.9 Falta envido.**
  - Both teams in **malas** → a querida falta envido **decides the partido**.
  - A team in **buenas** → it is worth the points the **leading** team needs to reach 30.
  - In **pica-pica** → it is worth **6 points in total**, whatever the previous cantos.
- **11.10 Partido ended by envido.** If the proof takes a team to 30, the partido ends at that moment; any pending truco, retruco or vale cuatro has no effect.
- **11.11 Truco sung before the envido.** If truco is sung while the envido can still be sung, the rival can **put the envido first**: the envido is settled and the truco stays pending (except for an out-of-turn canto that lapses, ch. 13).

## 12. Truco and irregular cantos

- **12.1 Sequence.** Truco → retruco → vale cuatro. As a general rule, only **the team that accepted** the previous level can raise.
- **12.2 Points.**

| Situation              | Points |
| ---------------------- | ------ |
| Mano without truco     | 1      |
| Truco no querido       | 1      |
| Truco querido          | 2      |
| Retruco no querido     | 2      |
| Retruco querido        | 3      |
| Vale cuatro no querido | 3      |
| Vale cuatro querido    | 4      |

- **12.3 Canto irregular.** A retruco or vale cuatro out of sequence or from a team without the right to raise. It is **validated** if the rival answers it validly or keeps playing without objecting in time; from then on it has the same effects as a regular canto.
- **12.4 Deadline to object.**
  - If the answer lets the mano continue → until the player whose turn it is plays their card.
  - If a "no quiero" ends the mano → before the team that sang releases its cards relying on that answer.
- **12.5 Express pardon.** In a mano redonda, both captains can cancel an accidental or irregular canto and return to the previous state (in pica-pica, the two players of the duelo). The state must be clear before continuing.

## 13. Cantos in turn and out of turn

- **13.1 In turn** is the player who is to play the next card.
- **13.2 Canto in turn.** The rival must settle it before play continues. It **does not lapse** because a card is played.
- **13.3 Envido + truco in turn with a card played.** The card stays played and both cantos still count. The envido is settled first, then the truco.
- **13.4 Canto fuera de turno.** An active player who is not in turn may sing envido or truco. The rival may answer now or leave it pending.
- **13.5 Lapsing.** If the rival does not answer and the player in turn **plays their card**, all unanswered out-of-turn cantos **lapse** (have no effect).
- **13.6 Several pending.** With envido and truco pending out of turn, if the rival answers, the envido is settled first. Then they can answer the truco or play their card to let it lapse.
- **13.7 "Sin ley de juego".** It does not exist in Se Pica TSA. The expression has no effect; valid cantos said along with it are assessed separately.

## Anexo B. Envido: values, sequences and points

### B.1 Basic values

Cards 1 to 7 are worth their number; figuras (10, 11, 12) are worth 0. Two cards of the same suit add 20 plus their values. Without a pair of the same suit, the highest numbered card counts.

### B.2 Sequences and points

| Canto                                        | Querido    | No querido |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Envido                                       | 2          | 1          |
| Real envido                                  | 3          | 1          |
| Falta envido                                 | Per score* | 1          |
| Envido – envido                              | 4          | 2          |
| Envido – real envido                         | 5          | 2          |
| Envido – falta envido                        | Per score* | 2          |
| Real envido – falta envido                   | Per score* | 3          |
| Envido – envido – falta envido               | Per score* | 4          |
| Envido – envido – real envido                | 7          | 4          |
| Envido – real envido – falta envido          | Per score* | 5          |
| Envido – envido – real envido – falta envido | Per score* | 7          |

\* See B.3.

Rule of thumb that matches the table: **no querido = the sum of what was already on the table before the last canto** (minimum 1). Querido without falta = the sum of each canto's value (envido = 2, real envido = 3).

### B.3 Falta envido

- **Mano redonda:** governed by 11.9. If both teams are in malas, it decides the partido; if either is in buenas, it is worth the points the leading team needs to reach 30.
- **Pica-pica:** worth **6 points in total**, whatever the previous invitations.
