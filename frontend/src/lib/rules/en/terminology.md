# Terminology

## 2. Official terminology

| Term                     | Definition                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ronda**                | Stage in which each active player plays one card in order. ("Baza" is not used.)                                        |
| **Mano**                 | Set of up to 3 rondas, until the winner and the points are settled.                                                     |
| **Ser mano**             | The player seated immediately to the right of the mazo; starts the first ronda.                                         |
| **Ser pie**              | The last player to act in the order.                                                                                    |
| **Mano redonda**         | A mano in which the active players of both teams play in the general order.                                             |
| **Pica-pica**            | A mano made up of 3 successive individual duelos between players with the same number.                                  |
| **Duelo de pica-pica**   | A 1 vs 1 contest within a pica-pica mano.                                                                               |
| **Active player**        | Can play, sing (cantar), answer, make señas and decide in the mano or duelo.                                            |
| **Tapar**                | Placing one's cards face down in the playing area and taking no further part in the mano (the cards stay identifiable). |
| **Irse al mazo**         | Leaving the mano/duelo by putting one's cards into the mazo, or with the valid regulation expression.                   |
| **Partido**              | A complete game to 30 points.                                                                                           |
| **Captain**              | The team's formal representative before the organization and the referee.                                               |
| **Verbal warning**       | A call to attention for a minor offence. It is not a sanction and does not change the score.                            |
| **Disciplinary warning** | A sanction counted against the team.                                                                                    |
| **Canto fuera de turno** | A canto by an active player who is not the one to play the next card.                                                   |
| **Canto irregular**      | A retruco or vale cuatro out of sequence, or sung by a team without the right to raise.                                 |
