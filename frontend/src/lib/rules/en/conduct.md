# Integrity and sanctions

## 18. Spectators, outside help and integrity

- **18.1** Spectators may not interfere; the referee may ask them to leave. The team is not sanctioned unless it took part or coordinated.
- **18.2 Accidental information.** Not sanctioned and, in general, the mano continues. It is only annulled if there was intentional intervention.
- **18.3 Deliberate outside help** → disciplinary warning; if there is coordination or fraud, a greater sanction.
- **18.4 Competitive integrity.** Forbidden: fixing results, losing on purpose, sharing information with the rival, agreeing cantos or plays between teams, manipulating results to favour or harm third parties, offering or receiving money or benefits, and betting (1.12). It may lead to **immediate disqualification**.

## 19. Warnings, sanctions and disqualification

### Scale

```
Minor irregularity  ──► Verbal warning (not a sanction, does not change the score)
        │ repetition
        ▼
Disciplinary warning (counted against the TEAM)
        │ 2nd in the same partido
        ▼
Loss of the partido (Win by Sanction for the rival)

Serious offence / fraud / integrity ──► Direct sanction, even immediate disqualification
```

- **19.1 Verbal warning** for minor offences without a special consequence. If repeated, it may become a disciplinary warning. Minor accidental and correctable irregularities do **not** trigger an automatic sanction. Dealing errors keep their own counter (6.4).
- **19.2 Disciplinary warnings.**
  - Counted against the **team**.
  - **2nd warning in the same partido → the team loses the partido.**
  - Accumulation across partidos, disqualification by accumulation and the counter reset between the qualifying and televised stages are defined by the **Particular Conditions**.
  - If the same warning is the 2nd of the partido and also grounds for accumulated disqualification → **both** consequences apply, and the rival records a **Win by Disqualification**.
  - The disciplinary regime remains in force until homologation.
- **19.3 Conduct that may lead to a disciplinary warning:**
  - Interference in a duelo de pica-pica.
  - Unauthorized señas, keywords, another language or codes.
  - Seeking or using outside information.
  - Improper use of phones, smartwatches, headphones or other devices.
  - Delaying play on purpose or repeatedly, or arriving late.
  - Refusing to comply with a referee's decision.
  - Serious disrespect, harassment, intimidation or unsporting conduct (short of disqualification).
  - Altering the scoreboard or the result record.
  - Deliberately changing the assigned positions.
  - Leaving the table without authorization.
  - Refusing without justification to validate the result in the official system.
  - Abusing claims to delay (a good-faith claim is not sanctioned).
  - Playing, touching cards, making señas or talking strategy during a suspension.
  - Betting or accessing betting platforms.
  - Depending on the circumstances, also: repeating handling already warned about, covering one's face, refusing to show everyone a card shown in private, or communicating between duelos de pica-pica.
- **19.4 Carteo, fraud and integrity.** Carteo = any deliberate manipulation of the mazo, cut, shuffle, deal or cards to gain an advantage (arranging, reserving, drawing from an unauthorized position, hiding, substituting or swapping cards). → Possible **immediate disqualification**, without prior warnings; the referee intervenes on their own initiative. The following may also lead to disqualification: organized outside help, agreed codes, collusion, match fixing, losing on purpose to benefit a third party, manipulating results and bribery.
- **19.5 Extreme conduct → immediate disqualification:** physical assault or attempted assault, serious threats, deliberate damage, fraud or carteo, collusion or fixing, endangering safety, and altering the positions again after a referee's correction.
- **19.6 Deliberate alteration of cards or evidence.** Nobody benefits from an irregularity they caused on purpose. Depending on the seriousness, the referee may: (a) restore the situation and apply a disciplinary warning; (b) give the rival **all the points at stake** in the mano or duelo; (c) declare the **loss of the partido** (Win by Sanction); (d) **disqualify**. If both teams commit extreme conduct → **Double Disqualification**.
- **19.7 Own regime.** These have their own consequence: dealer errors (6.4), concealment of a wrong number of cards (6.6), interference in pica-pica (15.6) and integrity. Minor matters without their own regime fall under 19.1.
- **19.8 Cannot be pardoned.** Teams cannot pardon serious integrity offences, fraud, carteo, collusion, organized outside help, assaults or threats. The referee may intervene even if nobody claims.
