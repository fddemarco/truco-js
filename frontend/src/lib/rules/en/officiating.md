# Scoring and officiating

## 16. Scoreboard, official system and homologation

- **16.1** Each team appoints a person responsible for the scoreboard.
- **16.2 Checking.** At the end of each mano, both teams check the points. In pica-pica the points are written down **at the end of each duelo**, but the joint check is done after the 3 duelos (or earlier, if someone reaches 30). Discrepancies must be raised **before the cut of the next mano** at the latest; after that, the result stands (except for a material error acknowledged by both or an integrity matter).
- **16.3 Official system.** The result is recorded in the official Se Pica TSA system; each captain accesses it from an authorized device.
- **16.4 Final validation.** Both captains validate → the result is **homologated**.
- **16.5 Disagreement.** If a captain disagrees or refuses to validate, the referee steps in, may review the physical scoreboard, the digital records and the cameras, and homologates. Refusing without justification → disciplinary sanction.
- **16.6 Technology failure.** It does not suspend the partido: play continues with a physical scoreboard or manual record, which is uploaded later.
- **16.7 Referee credentials.** The referee may homologate or correct results without a captain's acceptance.
- **16.8 Closing.** Homologation closes the sporting result. During the following **15 minutes** only **material or entry** errors may be corrected (wrongly entered score, wrong identification, recording failure); manos, cantos and claims are not reopened. Conduct that occurred before homologation can be sanctioned even if a team has already reached 30. What happens after homologation does not change the result, except for fraud or integrity (exceptional review).

## 17. Referee, claims and video review

- **17.1** The referee is the highest authority of the partido; their decisions on a play are final.
- **17.2** Only the **captain** formally requests intervention. The player involved may ask for a display or object within the game. In pica-pica, the contender's request to their captain suspends the duelo.
- **17.3 Suspension.** With a formal claim, play is suspended and **deadlines are frozen**. During the suspension nobody plays or touches cards, makes señas, sings, cuts or talks strategy. Regulation words said to explain the claim have no effect. Only the referee resumes play.
- **17.4** The claim may be raised at the table or in private; the decision is communicated to both teams.
- **17.5 Deadlines.** Only about current situations or those with a deadline still open. Closed manos are not reopened (except for a scoring error acknowledged by both or an integrity matter).
- **17.6 Cameras.** The referee may review official footage; if it is not enough, they decide with what they have. There is no automatic right to replay the mano.
- **17.7 On their own initiative.** As a general rule, the referee intervenes at a captain's request. They intervene on their own initiative when there are signs of carteo, fraud, collusion, assaults, threats, safety risks or serious integrity offences.
- **17.8 Chief Referee.** Higher authority for unforeseen cases, general matters and conflicts beyond a single partido. It is **not** an automatic appeal level.
- **17.9 Exceptional review** of earlier partidos affected by a serious or integrity offence: decided by the Chief Referee. A later disqualification alone is not enough to change earlier results.

## 20. Televised partidos

- **20.1** Same rules as any other partido.
- **20.2** Players may not receive information from monitors, commentators, production or anything from the broadcast.
- **20.3** Sporting decisions belong only to the referee or the Chief Referee; production has no refereeing powers.
- **20.4 Television Operating Protocol.** It covers only technical and production aspects and does not change the rules or the sanctions. Production may not interrupt a mano without the referee's authorization (except in an emergency).
- **20.5 Brands and clothing.** Third-party brands may not be displayed without written authorization (clause XVI of the Terms and Conditions). Clothing provided by the Organizer is mandatory; not wearing it may lead to being left out of the broadcast and to a disciplinary warning.
