# Pica-pica

## 15. Pica-pica

- **15.1 Start and alternation.**
  - It starts in the mano **after** the one in which a team **reaches or passes 5 points**.
  - From then on manos **alternate**: mano redonda, pica-pica mano, mano redonda...
  - If a team reaches or passes **25** during a pica-pica mano, the remaining duelos of that mano are still played; from the next one on, all manos are redondas.
  - If it reaches 25 in a mano redonda → all following manos are redondas.
  - If a team reaches **30** at any point → the partido ends immediately (the remaining duelos are not played).
- **15.2 Pairings.** 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2, 3 vs 3 (reserved numbering).
- **15.3 Dealing.** Same as in a mano redonda (3 cards to all 6 players). Cards are **not** dealt again between duelos. All 6 may look at their cards.
- **15.4 Order of the duelos.** The first is that of the player to the **right of the mazo** against the rival with the same number. Then follow the table order to the first player whose duelo has not been played yet (likewise for the 3rd). In each duelo, the mano is whoever comes first going right from the mazo.
- **15.5 Future duelos.** While a duelo is being played, the others have not started. Expressions by players of future duelos **have no effect** (and no sanction if accidental). Saying "me voy al mazo" has no effect, but **physically putting** the cards into the mazo takes the player out of their duelo. Tapar before one's duelo has no effect; the cards remain available.
- **15.6 Interference.** Players not in the current duelo may not make señas, advise, remind of cards, declare, answer or intervene. Accidentally exposing a card is not sanctioned; doing it on purpose to inform is interference.
  - **One** team interferes → the duelo continues and **all the points at stake in that duelo** go to the offender's rival, plus the disciplinary sanction.
  - **Both** interfere → the duelo is annulled (0–0, not repeated) and both are sanctioned. If the sanctions would make both lose the partido, the one who interfered **first** loses by sanction (the other's sanction is recorded). The other duelos continue if the partido goes on.
  - **Between duelos** general words of encouragement are allowed ("dale", "jugá", "tranquilo"). Commenting on cards seen, giving specific strategic instructions, making señas or pushing claims from a finished duelo are **not** allowed → disciplinary warning (the points of the next duelo are not awarded automatically).
- **15.7 Tapar or irse al mazo.** The general rules apply. The cards can be shown to prove the envido while they remain identifiable. If a canto is pending, it is a **rejection**.
- **15.8 Falta envido** = 6 points in total.
- **15.9 Proof and claims.** The rival in the duelo may claim until the **effective start of the next duelo** (when one of its players plays a card, sings envido or truco, tapa or se va al mazo). If the winner cannot prove it → all the points of the duelo go to the rival. In the **last duelo**, the deadline is the cut of the next mano.
- **15.10 Incidents.** Settled **only by the two contenders** (neither captains nor teammates intervene). If one asks for the referee, they tell their captain and the duelo is **suspended at that moment**; the captain formalizes it without delay. 17.3 applies.
