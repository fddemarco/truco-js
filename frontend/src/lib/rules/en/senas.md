# Communication and señas

## 14. Communication, language, talk and señas

- **14.1 Language.** Spanish. Cantos, answers and declarations only count in Spanish with the recognized expressions. Isolated foreign words in common use are allowed, as long as they are not a canto or a code. Using another language to hide information = unauthorized code → disciplinary warning.
- **14.2 Talk.** In a mano redonda teammates may talk freely. Lying, chicana and picardía are allowed, within the limits of respect and integrity.
- **14.3 Forbidden codes.** Words, numbers, gestures, objects or patterns agreed in advance to pass on card information may not be used.
- **14.4 Official señas.** Only those in **Anexo C**, and they may be used truthfully or falsely. Invented or altered señas are forbidden. Covering one's face so the señas cannot be seen is not allowed: first a verbal warning, then a disciplinary one.

## Anexo C. Official Se Pica TSA señas

### C.1 Rule of use

- Only the señas in this annex are valid. A seña may be **true or false**: making it does not mean having that card or combination.
- Using other gestures, words, objects or agreed patterns, or modifying an official seña to create an additional code, is forbidden.
- Covering one's face or preventing the rivals from seeing the señas is not allowed.
- "Right" and "left" are from the perspective of the player making the seña.
- If there are illustrations in the future, they are for teaching only: in case of any difference, the text prevails.

### C.2 Official list

| No. | Card / situation                          | Gesture                                  |
| --- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 01  | As de espadas (ancho de espadas)          | Raise both eyebrows                      |
| 02  | As de bastos (ancho de bastos)            | Wink the right eye                       |
| 03  | Siete de espadas (siete bravo)            | Move the corner of the lips to the right |
| 04  | Siete de oros                             | Move the corner of the lips to the left  |
| 05  | Any tres                                  | Gently bite the lower lip                |
| 06  | Any dos                                   | Purse and push the lips forward (kiss)   |
| 07  | Anchos falsos (as de copas or as de oros) | Open the mouth slightly                  |
| 08  | Any sota (10)                             | Touch the wrist                          |
| 09  | Any caballo (11)                          | Touch the elbow                          |
| 10  | Any rey (12)                              | Touch the shoulder                       |
| 11  | No good cards for the truco               | Close both eyes                          |
| 12  | Good envido                               | Wrinkle the nose                         |

### C.3 Scope

- Cards or situations not on the list (for example 7 de copas or de bastos, 4, 5 and 6) **have no authorized seña**.
- There is no seña for flor, since flor is not played.
- In pica-pica, only the two players of the current duelo may make señas. A player not in the duelo may not use a seña to pass information to their teammate.
