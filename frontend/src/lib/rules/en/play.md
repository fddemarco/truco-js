# Playing a mano

## 7. Playing the mano and the rondas

- **7.1** The mano player plays first; play then moves to the **right**.
- **7.2** The highest card wins the ronda (Anexo A). The winner starts the next ronda.
- **7.3 Tie between teammates.** If two or more players of the **same team** tie with the highest card, the ronda goes to their team; the next one is started by whichever of them played first.
- **7.4 Inactive winner.** If the player who should start is no longer active, the player immediately to their right starts (whatever their team).
- **7.5** Finished rondas keep their result even if a player becomes inactive afterwards.
- **7.6 Pardas** (a tie between cards of rival teams):

| Ronda 1 | Ronda 2 | Ronda 3 | Mano goes to                    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------------------------------- |
| Parda   | X wins  | —       | X                               |
| Parda   | Parda   | X wins  | X                               |
| Parda   | Parda   | Parda   | The team of the **mano** player |
| X wins  | Parda   | —       | X                               |
| X wins  | Y wins  | Parda   | X (the winner of the 1st)       |
| X wins  | Y wins  | Z wins  | Z                               |
| X wins  | X wins  | —       | X                               |

After a parda ronda, the next ronda is started by the **mano** player of that mano.

- **7.7 Played cards.** They stay face up and separate until the end of the mano, so the order can be reconstructed. Deliberately handling them → verbal warning, and disciplinary if repeated; hiding information → 19.6.

## 8. Tapar, irse al mazo and inactive players

- **8.1 Tapar.** Placing a card face down **inside the playing area** (outside the area it does not count). Tapar one card means tapar all of them. From that moment the player is **inactive**: their cantos, answers and señas do not count. The covered cards stay identifiable and can be shown to prove the envido.
- **8.2 Accidental drop on the table.**
  - Face down (inside or outside the area) → the player picks it up, saying it was accidental; it does not count as played or tapada.
  - Face up **inside** the area → it counts as **played**.
  - Face up **outside** the area → the player may pick it up.
- **8.3 Irse al mazo.** By saying "me voy al mazo" when the expression is valid, or by physically putting the cards into the mazo. The player becomes inactive. If they only said it and their cards are still identifiable, they may show them **only to prove the envido**. Once the cards are in the mazo, no longer.
- **8.4 Collective withdrawal.** "Nos vamos al mazo", said by an **active** player in a mano redonda, withdraws the **whole team**. Said by an inactive player, it does not count.
- **8.5 Pending canto.** In a mano redonda, if **one** player tapa or se va al mazo, that is not the team's answer: active teammates can still answer. A **collective** withdrawal does count as **rejecting** the canto. In pica-pica, tapar or irse al mazo **is a rejection** of the pending canto.

## 9. Cards played, shown, dropped and lost

- **9.1 Played card.** Deliberately placed in the playing area and released, or dropped face up inside the area (8.2). It cannot be taken back. While still holding it, the player may show it and change it.
- **9.2 Dropped on the floor.** It does not count as played even if it was seen; if recovered, it goes back to the player. If it had already been played and bounces to the floor, it is still played.
- **9.3 Lost and unrecoverable card.** The player becomes **inactive** for the rest of the mano (their teammates continue; rondas already won stand). They can only prove the envido with the cards they still hold, and the lost card is not presumed. At the end of the mano the mazo is replaced. If it was accidental there is no sanction; if deliberate, there is. If the card turns up later, it is not brought back.
- **9.4 Played card that disappears.** An attempt is made to reconstruct it (agreement, position, video). If that is not possible and it was accidental, the referee may annul the mano. If deliberate, nobody benefits and ch. 19 applies.
- **9.5 Card played out of turn.** It stays played and visible. Play follows the normal order; when the offender's turn comes, they are considered to have already played that card.
- **9.6 Two cards played.** The order in which they landed counts. If simultaneous, the player declares which came first and that stands.
- **9.7 Public display.** An active player may show their cards, always to the **whole** table.
- **9.8 Private display** (to a teammate only): forbidden. That card must be shown to everyone; refusing → disciplinary warning (the points do not change). If it was done to pass information → ch. 19. Nobody may turn over or expose a rival's cards.
